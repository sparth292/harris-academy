About Us

Our story, our mission, our art

Our Mission

Harris.Academy was founded with a single mission: to make authentic martial arts training accessible to anyone with the dedication to learn. We believe that the discipline, focus, and resilience developed through martial arts training can transform lives.

Our instructors bring decades of combined experience across multiple disciplines including Jeet Kune Do, Filipino Martial Arts (Kali, Silat, Pananjakman), Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, and tactical self-defense systems. We don't just teach techniques — we teach principles that adapt to any situation.

- The Harris.Academy Team


Discipline

Build mental and physical discipline through structured training programs.

Respect

Every martial art is rooted in respect for yourself, your partners, and the tradition.

Growth

Continuous improvement is the goal — there is always something new to learn.
