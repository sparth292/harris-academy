Self-Defense Fundamentals

A complete beginner's guide to personal safety

Welcome to Self-Defense Fundamentals, the foundational course in the Harris.Academy training curriculum. This course is designed for absolute beginners and covers the essential skills needed to protect yourself in real-world situations.

Self-defense is not about fighting — it's about avoiding danger when possible and escaping when necessary. Throughout this course, you'll learn the critical difference between self-defense and combat sports, and why the mindset of avoidance and de-escalation is your most powerful tool.

What You'll Learn

Situational Awareness: The most important self-defense skill is noticing danger before it arrives. Learn how to read environments, identify threats, and maintain safe distances.

Basic Striking: Proper fist formation, stance, and fundamental strikes including the straight punch, palm heel, and elbow strikes. These simple tools form the backbone of physical self-defense.

Escape Techniques: How to break free from common grabs, holds, and chokes. These techniques prioritize escape over engagement.

Verbal De-escalation: Often overlooked but critical — learn how to use your voice to defuse potentially violent situations before they become physical.

Course Structure

This course is divided into 8 progressive modules, each building on the previous. Each module includes instructional video, practice drills, and a knowledge check. You can progress at your own pace, and all materials are available for lifetime access once enrolled.

By the end of this course, you'll have a solid foundation in personal safety and the confidence to handle common threatening situations. Remember: the best fight is the one you avoid.
